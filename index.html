    Before you pay a deposit

    • Developer licence. Every developer needs a qualification licence (Proclamation 1357/2024).
    • Escrow only. Your money goes into escrow and is released stage by stage once each is verified.
    • The 80% rule. A unit can't be transferred before 80% completion unless you both agree.
    • Title and permit. See the land deed and building permit for the actual plot.
    • Repricing clauses. Look for any wording that lets the developer raise the price for input costs.

    Moving home, partly or fully

    The rules as of November 2026. Each guide shows the date it was last checked.

    Yellow Card

    Live and work without a visa, residence permit or work permit. Invest as a domestic investor and own property.

    Household goods

    Personal effects used for 12+ months come in duty-free with proof of a year or more abroad. Vehicles are excluded.

    Bringing a car

    Petrol and diesel car imports have been banned since January 2024, and the returnee route is closed. Fully built EVs pay 15% duty.

    Starting a business

    Duty-free capital goods for approved projects. Diaspora traders can import under the new Franco Valuta rules.

    Buying a home

    Diaspora home loans of up to 80% are on offer, and the scorecard above shows who delivers.

    Banking and cash

    Open a non-resident foreign-currency account. Staying past 90 days? Deposit your foreign cash there within 90 days of arrival. Once you live here, you have 30 days to convert.

    Tax on foreign pensions and salaries is still being reviewed with a tax adviser and will be published once checked.
