    Visiting? Change cash at a bureau

    Licensed bureaus pay about 14% more than the NBE rate for cash dollars, close to the parallel market but legal and with a receipt.

    Staying more than 90 days? Foreign cash you still hold must go into a non-resident foreign-currency account within 90 days of your entry stamp. People living in Ethiopia have 30 days to convert.

    Moving home · partly or fully

    The rules, in plain words

    As of November 2026. Confirm with a lawyer or tax adviser before acting.

    Yellow Card

    Live and work with no visa, residence or work permit; invest as a domestic investor and own property. You can’t vote or work in defence, security or foreign service.

    Proclamation 270/2002

    Household goods, duty-free

    Used for 12+ months, one of each electronic item, one shipment within 6 months of arrival. Needs proof of a year or more abroad and an embassy supporting letter.

    Directive 923/2014

    At the airport

    Duty-free for travellers: 2 litres of alcohol, 500 ml of perfume, 2 phones, 1 laptop, 1 camera, and your own medicines.

    Directive 923/2014

    Bringing a car

    Petrol and diesel imports banned since January 2024; the returnee car privilege ended in August 2023. Electric cars: 15% duty fully built, 5% semi-assembled.

    Customs Commission · Ministry of Transport

    Pets

    Microchip, rabies vaccine given 30 days to 12 months before travel, a health certificate, and a Ministry of Agriculture import permit before you fly.

    Ministry of Agriculture

    Fayda ID

    Free 12-digit digital ID, registered in person at a centre in Ethiopia. Needed for banks, SIM cards, title deeds, schools and health services.

    National ID Program

    Starting a business

    Duty-free capital goods and tax holidays for approved projects. With an investment licence, personal effects up to USD 10,000 FOB come in duty-free.

    Investment rules · FVD/01/2026

    Driving licence

    Authenticate your foreign licence, then convert it at the transport licensing office. An international permit isn’t enough once you live here.

    Transport licensing

    Banking and cash

    Open a non-resident foreign-currency account. Staying past 90 days? Deposit foreign cash there within 90 days of arrival.
