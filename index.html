Visiting? Change cash at a bureau

Licensed bureaus pay about 14% more than the NBE rate for cash dollars, close to the parallel market but legal and with a receipt.

Staying more than 90 days? Foreign cash you still hold must go into a non-resident foreign-currency account within 90 days of your entry stamp. People living in Ethiopia have 30 days to convert.

Some bureau selling rates exclude a 0.5–2% service fee. CBE Connect and telebirr Remit take card payments but don’t publish their rates yet. We’ve asked them to.

Moving home · partly or fully

The rules, in plain words

As of November 2026. Confirm with a lawyer or tax adviser before acting.

Yellow Card

Live and work with no visa, residence or work permit. Invest as a domestic investor and own property.

Proclamation 270/2002

Household goods, duty-free

Personal effects used for 12+ months. Needs proof of a year or more abroad (passport stamps or an embassy letter). Vehicles are excluded.

Directive 923/2014

Bringing a car

Petrol and diesel imports have been banned since January 2024, and the returnee route was closed. Electric vehicles only: 15% duty if fully built.

Rules as of Nov 2026

Starting a business

Duty-free capital goods for approved projects. Diaspora traders can import under the Franco Valuta rules.

FVD/01/2026

Buying a home

Yellow Card holders can own property. Foreign nationals can’t borrow locally. Diaspora home loans of up to 80% are on offer (Wegagen with Gift).

Proclamation 1388/2025

Banking and cash

Open a non-resident foreign-currency account. Staying past 90 days? Deposit foreign cash there within 90 days of arrival.
